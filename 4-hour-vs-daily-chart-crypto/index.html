Is the 4-Hour Chart Better Than the Daily for Crypto? 5 Years Tested

REVERSED.

This test re-runs an earlier research note that found daily bars edged 4-hour bars for a long-only Donchian breakout system on BTC and ETH, using identical rules on both timeframes from 2021-09-16 to 2026-09-16. The result reversed: the 4-hour chart made +$82,798 against +$54,489, with MAR 0.64 vs 0.32 and a 9.4% vs 12.7% maximum drawdown, and every 4-hour setting beat every daily setting. The original note was wrong because its 4-hour Ethereum download stopped at 2021-04-28, about four months of a five-year test. The win is thinner than it looks: Ethereum supplied +$54,280 of the total, Bitcoin's single best trade was +$43,078, and the 4-hour made 107 trades to the daily's 37 at the same per-trade cost. It is one five-year sample, 49% of it in a bull regime.
