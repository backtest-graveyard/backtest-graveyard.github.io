The email list: what's kept and how to leave

The Backtest Graveyard — last updated 27 November 2026

What's collected

  • Your email address. That's the only thing you type in.
  • When you signed up, and on which page. So there's a record that you asked for it.
  • Which version of the sign-up wording you saw. Same reason.
  • A scrambled (salted, one-way hashed) copy of your IP address. Used only to stop one machine from flooding the form. The real IP is never stored. Sign-up attempts used for that limit are deleted after 24 hours.

Why

To send you what the form offers: the receipts notebook and the next test before it airs. Nothing else.

Who else sees it

Nobody buys it and nobody is given it. The list is never sold, rented, traded or shared. It's stored with Cloudflare, which runs the sign-up form's backend, and when an email goes out your address passes through the service that delivers it. That's the whole list of who touches it.

How to leave

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off. No login and no “are you sure” maze. You can also reply to any email and say unsubscribe. Either way it's done within 10 business days, usually at once.

Want your address deleted outright rather than just unsubscribed? Reply to any email and say so.

Not financial advice
