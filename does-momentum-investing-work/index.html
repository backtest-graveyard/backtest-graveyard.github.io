Does Momentum Investing Work? 16 Years Tested

JUST BETA.

This test runs textbook cross-sectional momentum (rank by 12-1 or 6-1 month return, rebalance monthly) on 29 liquid ETFs, mega-cap stocks and two coins from 2010-01-04 to 2026-06-29. The dollar-neutral long/short versions failed at MAR 0.12 and 0.32. Long-only top-third versions nominally passed at MAR 0.55 and 0.58, but with drawdowns of -69.2% and -68.3%, and the top 5 names (led by BTC and NVDA) were 42% of gross profit. Removing the five best months dropped the best version to MAR 0.44, and removing Bitcoin and Ethereum made all four versions fail. What remained was concentrated exposure to whatever had already gone up.
