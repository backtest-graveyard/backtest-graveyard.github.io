Known limitations, disclosed in the book

  • One figure has no reproducible source — the honest common-window MAR of 1.18 came from a script that no longer exists.Flagged in Chapter 22 and Appendix E.
  • Residual look-ahead in the gap screen inflates Chapter 11's rebuilt figures by an unknown amount.Accepted and disclosed rather than fixed; the strategy is rejected on concentration regardless.
  • Universe survivorship is only partially corrected in the value screener.The five-year filing-history filter removes recent listings, not established companies added to an index mid-window.
  • Some samples are small and say so — the MACD probe is 22 days and 36 trades.
