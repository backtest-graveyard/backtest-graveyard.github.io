How Much Backtesting Is Enough? 11 Strategies Tested

NOT THE QUESTION.

This episode runs no new backtest; it collects the six checks that the channel's first eleven strategies failed, and argues that the amount of data is not the real question. The bar is MAR of at least 0.5 and Sortino of at least 1.0, net of double the measured trading costs, on unseen data, and still standing after the five best trades are deleted. Deleting the top five trades took gap-and-go from MAR 1.56 to -0.30 and stat-arb from 0.94 to -0.30; in gap-and-go, 3 of 537 trades were 92% of the profit. Fifteen stat-arb spreads looked perfect in-sample and zero survived on data they had never seen. The useful number of years is whatever span contains the conditions that would kill the strategy.
