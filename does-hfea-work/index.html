Does HFEA (55% UPRO, 45% TMF) Work? 60 Years Tested

THE HEDGE BROKE.

This test examines HFEA, 55% UPRO (3x S&P 500) and 45% TMF (3x long Treasuries) rebalanced quarterly, on the real funds from 2016-01-04 to 2026-09-17 and in a rate-aware simulation from 1962. In the real window HFEA turned $1 into $4.65 against $4.46 for the S&P 500, but with a -70.5% drawdown against -33.8%, and 1,725 days underwater that were still open at the end of the data. In 2022 both legs fell together (UPRO -56.8%, TMF -72.6%, HFEA -64.1%) as the stock/bond correlation moved from -0.34 in 2016-2019 to +0.08. Through the 1962-1981 rising-rate era the simulation made +68.9% against +272.1% for plain stocks. Unleveraged 60/40 had the best MAR in both windows (0.47 real, 0.29 simulated).
