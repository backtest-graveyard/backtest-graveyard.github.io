Is TQQQ a Good Long-Term Investment? 10 Years Tested

WORSE RIDE.

This test compares buying and holding TQQQ (3x daily QQQ) with plain QQQ on Alpaca SIP total-return data from 2016-01-04 to 2026-09-16, a window that omits TQQQ's 2010-2015 run. TQQQ returned +3,097% against QQQ's +596%, with the higher CAGR (38.2% vs 19.9%), yet failed the channel's bar at MAR 0.47 while QQQ passed at 0.57. Its maximum drawdown was -81.7%, which needs a +445% gain to recover, and it spent 1,111 days underwater. A rule that held TQQQ only while QQQ was above its 200-day average reached MAR 0.65 with a -55.9% drawdown. Leverage raised the ending number but made the ride much worse.
