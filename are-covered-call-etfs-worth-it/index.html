Are Covered Call ETFs Like QYLD and JEPI Worth It? Tested

YOUR OWN MONEY.

This test compares five covered-call income ETFs (QYLD, XYLD, RYLD, JEPI, JEPQ) with their own underlying indexes on Alpaca SIP data through 2026-09-17, using both total return and share price. Every fund trailed its index: $10,000 in QYLD grew to $26,315 while the same amount in QQQ grew to $70,742. QYLD's share price fell -19.9% (from $23.12 to $18.51) while it paid about 12.8% a year, so part of the yield was returned capital. Taking the same $12,412 of income by selling QQQ shares instead left $33,015 against QYLD's $8,006, a difference of +$25,009. The funds did deliver lower volatility (QYLD 15.4% vs QQQ 22.2%), but the income was partly the holder's own money.
