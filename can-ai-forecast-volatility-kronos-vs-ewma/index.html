Can an AI Model Forecast Volatility? Kronos vs a 1990s Formula

BEATEN BY A FORMULA.

Kronos, an open-source transformer trained on about 12 billion K-lines, was tested as a volatility forecaster against a trailing 30-day estimator and the one-line EWMA(0.94) formula. Across 9 assets and 409 evaluation points at a 10-day horizon, Kronos posted an MAE of 0.553 versus 0.137 for both baselines, and a QLIKE of 0.706 versus 0.268 for EWMA. It lost to both baselines on all 9 assets, worst on NVDA at QLIKE 1.42 versus 0.19. Its RMSE of 1.216 reflects occasional wildly wrong forecasts that would size a position 4-5x wrong. The useful finding was the formula: EWMA(0.94) beat the trailing-30-day estimator in 36/36 cells on pooled QLIKE.
