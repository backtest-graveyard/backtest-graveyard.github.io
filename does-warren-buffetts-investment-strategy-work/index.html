Does Warren Buffett's Investment Strategy Work as a Stock Screener? 10 Years Tested

NOT VALIDATED.

A stock screener built on Warren Buffett's rules was backtested on today's S&P 500 (503 names) from 2015-01-02 to 2025-01-02. Each company had to clear hard gates first (ROE of at least 10%, debt/equity at most 2.0, positive earnings and free cash flow), then a quality score and a price score were multiplied, and the top 15 were held at equal weight for a year. The headline beat SPY: 21.44% a year against 13.00%, or +595.6% against +238.7%, with a MAR of 0.61 against a bar of 0.50. Removing the best 5 names dropped MAR to 0.47, under the bar. The #2 contributor was Coinbase, which wasn't in the index in any year it was held. Requiring 5 years of filing history put MAR at 0.50, and 0.43 without the best 5. The universe is survivorship-biased, so these results are an upper bound.
