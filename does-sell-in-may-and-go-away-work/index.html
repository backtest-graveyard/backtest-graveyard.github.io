Does “Sell in May and Go Away” Work? 100 Years of Data

STAY IN MAY.

This test runs the Sell in May rule (hold stocks November through April, T-bills May through October) on 100.1 years of Fama-French daily US total-market returns from 1926-07-01 to 2026-07-31. Winter did beat summer by about 3.8% a year, but summer still averaged +3.9% and was positive in 73% of years. The rule turned $1 into about $3,502 against buy-and-hold's about $17,630, keeping 19.9% of the ending wealth, in exchange for a smaller maximum drawdown (-58.9% vs -84.1%). Removing just three summers (1930, 1931, 2008) erased 26.8% of the seasonal gap. The season is real, but trading it gave up most of the money.
